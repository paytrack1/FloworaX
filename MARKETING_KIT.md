- Caption: *"One screen. Money in. Money out. Wetin remain. Free to start — reply 'PROFIT' and I go set am up for you."*

## 6. CapCut assembly checklist (the 45s video)

1. Export `endcard.html` → PNG (last frame)
2. Record app screen: register → dashboard → New Sale → booking link (Scenes 2–4)
3. Optional: 1 free-credit AI b-roll clip for Scene 1 (market/salon)
4. CapCut: fast cuts every 2–4s, auto-captions ON, bold yellow text overlays on navy
5. Beat: CapCut library Afrobeats-adjacent track
6. Export 1080×1920, 30fps MP4
7. Post: WhatsApp Status, TikTok, IG Reels, Facebook groups (Nigerian SME / church business groups — read group rules first)

---

## 7. The weekly scorecard (paper, 5 lines)

For week 1: **sent → replied → installed → recorded first sale → came back day 3**

If installs are high but first-sales are low → the onboarding habit is the problem, fix the follow-up, not the app.
If replies are low → the DM/status copy is the problem, change the hook, not the channel.
