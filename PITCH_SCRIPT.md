# FloworaX Pitch & Onboarding Script

Built for how Nigerian micro-businesses and churches actually decide to use software:
someone they trust shows them, on their own phone, with their own money numbers.

---

## 1. The 30-second pitch (elevator / market / after service)

> "You know how you write today's sales in a book, and by month end you can't
> tell if you made profit or just moved money around? This app shows you
> **exactly** what came in, what went out, and what you actually kept — plus
> your customers can book appointments and pay you by card. It works on your
> phone, even data no dey. First month free — let me set it up for you now."

**Rule: never explain features. Ask a question first:**

- Trader/shop: "How do you know if this month beat last month — profit, or just cash passing through?"
- Salon/therapist: "When clients book you on WhatsApp, how do you remember who's coming tomorrow?"
- Church: "After counting offering, how long before you know this month's total against last month?"

The answer to that question IS your demo. Open the app and fix *that* problem in front of them.

---

## 2. The WhatsApp pitch (copy-paste, send to your first 10 people)

> Hello sir/ma 🙌
>
> I built an app for businesses like yours — it tracks your sales and
> expenses, shows your real profit, and your customers can book & pay you
> by card.
>
> No big talk — I'll set it up for you free and enter last month's numbers
> with you, so you can see your profit on screen in 10 minutes.
>
> Can I come show you tomorrow, or send me one day's sales and I'll show you?
>
> [Your referral link] — created from my link, so you help me out too 🙏

Attach: one screenshot of the dashboard with real-looking numbers. Never more than one message before they reply.

---

## 3. The 20-minute in-person install (this is the whole product)

Come with their permission, sit down, and do it WITH them — they touch the phone:

**Minute 0–3 — Setup**
1. Open your referral link on THEIR phone → they register (their email, business name)
2. Enter the OTP from their email together → verified ✓
3. Pick business type together (this shapes their whole app)

**Minute 3–10 — Load their history (the hook)**
4. Add their services/products (ask: "what do you sell?")
5. Enter the last 3–5 days of sales from their book/WhatsApp
6. Enter 2–3 expenses ("data, transport, restocking…")

**Minute 10–13 — The moment that sells it**
7. Open the Dashboard together → point at the profit line:
   *"This is what you actually kept. Your book doesn't show you this."*
8. Show Reports → this month vs the trend

**Minute 13–17 — The features they'll use tomorrow**
9. Record ONE new sale live — cash first, then show card payment via Paystack
10. Add a customer → show the booking link: "send this to clients, they pick a time and pay"

**Minute 17–20 — The close**
11. "Keep using it free — record today's sales tonight before you sleep. That's it, one habit."
12. Show where Notifications and Reports are. Leave.

**Follow-up (next day, one message):**
> "Hope you entered yesterday's sales 😄 — anything confusing, send me a screenshot."

---

## 4. Objection handling (the only 4 you'll hear)

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I don't have data" | "It works offline — record sales without data, it syncs when you connect. Recording one sale uses less data than one WhatsApp status." |
| "Is my money safe? Is this a bank?" | "It doesn't hold your money. Card payments go straight to YOUR Paystack/bank account. The app is just your record book, but smarter." |
| "I'll just use my book" | "Show me your profit for last month from the book. *(pause)* The app shows it in one second." |
| "How much is it?" | "Free now. When your business grows past 50 sales a month, Paid is ₦7,000 — but if it's paying for itself you won't ask me that." |

---

## 5. The church version (if your first 3 are churches)

Same script, but:
- Demo with the **Bulk Offering** flow on their own counting day
- The hook line: *"Counting done by 10am, totals on the treasurer's phone, and the monthly comparison does itself."*
- One happy pastor = a whole congregation of new users. Ask for a 30-second testimony you can forward.
- NEVER demo with real offering amounts on screen in public — use their own phone, private.

---

## 6. Track your pilot (paper is fine)

For each of your 10 users write one line: name · installed date · entered sales
yesterday? (Y/N) · would they pay ₦7k? (their words, not a guess)

**The metric that decides everything: did they record sales on day 3 and day 7?**
Users who record twice in the first week stay. Users who don't, won't — no matter
what the app does. Your job in month one is driving that habit, not adding features.
